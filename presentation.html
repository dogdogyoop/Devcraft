01 / The challenge

The
Problem

Developers lack a single platform that combines social interaction, coding, learning, and career growth. Existing tools are fragmented and focus on different parts of the developer experience.

Fragmented Tools

  • General social media is not focused on developers.
  • GitHub is strong for code, but limited socially.
  • LinkedIn is good for careers, but not coding communities.

The Gap

  • Developers switch between multiple platforms to share, learn, connect, and find opportunities.
